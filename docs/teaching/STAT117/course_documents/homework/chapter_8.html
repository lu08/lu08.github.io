Exercise 8.14

If a single score is selected at random from a standard normal distribution, find the probability that:

  1. The score is above \(z = 1.36\).
  2. The score is above \(z = -2.08\).
  3. The score is below \(z = 1.36\).
  4. The score is below \(z = -2.08\).
  5. The score is between \(z = -2.08\) and \(z = 1.36\).
  6. The score is between \(z = .40\) and \(z = 1.36\).
  7. The score is between \(z = -.40\) and \(z = -1.36\).
  8. The score is less than \(z = -.40\) or greater than \(z = .40\).
Show solution
  1. .09
  2. .98
  3. .91
  4. .02
  5. .89
  6. .26
  7. .26
  8. .69

Exercise 8.26

  1. How many of these days had maximum daily temperatures above 80 degrees Fahrenheit?
  2. What would be the percentile rank for a day that had a maximum temperature of 80 degrees Fahrenheit?
Show solution
  1. 990 days.
  2. Approximately 1 percent.

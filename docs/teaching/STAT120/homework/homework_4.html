  1. What are the top 10 platforms in terms of number of games released? How many games were released on each of those 10 platforms? What are the average critic scores of the games on the 10 platforms (ignoring games with missing scores)? What was the median release year for games on each of the 10 platforms (ignoring games with missing release year)?
Show solution 
platforms <- games %>%
  group_by(Platform) %>%
  summarize(
    number_of_games = n(),
    average_critic_score = mean(Critic_Score, na.rm = TRUE),
    median_release_year = median(Year_of_Release, na.rm = TRUE)
  ) %>%
  top_n(10, number_of_games)
